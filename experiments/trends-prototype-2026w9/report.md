# Running-back trends: offline prototype

Generated by `npm run trends:rb`. Research output, not a published valuation. No market price is compared and no player is labelled buy-low or sell-high.

## How to read it

- **Ledger 1, observed.** Box-score and snap facts by window: 2025 season, 2026 Weeks 1–5, 2026 Weeks 6–9. Each row is tagged **input** if challenger F reads it, or **context** if F does not. A context row never explains a projection change.
- **Ledger 2, projection.** Challenger F (research) evaluated at two decision instants: after Weeks 1–5 (2026-10-13T12:15:00.000Z) and after Weeks 6–9 (2026-11-10T13:15:00.000Z). F projects a season as 17 × expected games share × expected PPR points per game played. Both inputs are recency-weighted over 2023–2026 (each season counts half the next). Between the two instants F's fitted lines and age term do not change, so its projection moves only through those two inputs. The split below is exact: the parts add to the total.
- **Value** is two-season points above replacement (RB35). It can also move when *other* players' projections move the replacement level: earlier 71.3 / 47.1, later 70.0 / 48.9 points for seasons 1 / 2.
- **Kinds.** *Observed*: a value the provider publishes as-is (a count, a snap percentage, an injury-report status). *Derived*: exact arithmetic on observed values (per-game rates, shares, PPR points under the site's scoring); no estimation. *Estimated*: a model output (everything F produces).
- **Error.** In the exploratory backtest F's season-points projections for running backs missed by 31.8 PPR points on average (calibrated mean absolute error, 2022–2023 validation dates). That describes the size of a typical miss on a season total. It is not a confidence interval, and it is not a threshold for deciding whether a change in the projection matters. F has no measured error for changes between two dates within a season.
- **Data through** the game kicking off 2026-11-10T01:15:00.000Z. The earlier instant is reconstructed from today's files, so any provider correction to Weeks 1–5 since then is already in it.
